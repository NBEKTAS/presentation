5.1 Operational roadmap

4-phase municipal execution

Sequential
Phase IFoundational risk understandingComp. 1 →

Dynamic multi-hazard assessments (PSHA, site effects), critical asset exposure, and GIS-based Common Operating Picture [32–36].

Phase IIAdaptive & integrated governanceComp. 2 →

Establish a City-Level Resilience Platform, enforce seismic codes and zoning, formalize cross-sector MoUs [36–38].

Phase IIIPrioritize & invest in DRRComp. 3 →

Allocate capital via CBA, CEA & MCA; structural retrofits and ecosystem-based mitigation funded through blended PPPs [33,39,40].

Phase IVPreparedness & recovery (BBB)Comp. 4 →

All-hazard Early Warning Systems and continuous drills; pre-disaster recovery blueprints guided by Build Back Better [40–44].

5.2 Governance & fiscal

Strategic mainstreaming levers

Institutional
Strategic urban master plansStatutory

Embed DRR into statutory planning cycles, binding land-use regulations, and long-term spatial master plans [32,45].

Ex-ante fiscal budgetingCapital lines

Institutionalize annual capital budget lines for structural & non-structural DRR — away from reactive crisis funding [32].

Accountability & auditingOversight

Performance auditing and resilience KPIs ensure policy durability across electoral cycles [32].

Multi-scalar harmonizationİRAP

Harmonize city actions with provincial frameworks and the Turkish İRAP & Earthquake Master Plans [45,46].

2 dimensions
